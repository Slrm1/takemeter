# r/nfl Annotation Guide

## analysis

Use when the author makes a broader football claim and supports it with meaningful evidence or causal football reasoning.

Signals can include scheme, coverage, pass protection, route concepts, personnel packages, usage, efficiency statistics, injury context, roster construction, or historical comparison.

## hot_take

Use when the author makes a broader confident claim, ranking, prediction, or evaluation but gives little meaningful support.

The opinion can be right or popular and still be a hot take.

## reaction

Use when the comment mainly expresses an immediate emotional, humorous, celebratory, or frustrated response to a play, score, penalty, injury, result, or news item.

## Three-Step Rule

1. Immediate event response with no broader argument -> reaction
2. Broader claim with meaningful support or reasoning -> analysis
3. Broader claim without meaningful support -> hot_take

## Important Reminders

- Do not label based on whether you agree.
- Do not label based on upvotes.
- Do not label based on team flair.
- Length alone does not determine the label.
- Profanity or capitalization alone does not determine the label.
- A statistic counts as analysis only when it actually supports reasoning.
- If a comment is purely factual, a pure question, bot output, deleted, or not football discussion, exclude it rather than forcing a label.

## Difficult-Case Note Format

When a row is hard, put a brief note in notes, for example:

analysis vs hot_take - specific pressure-rate evidence supports coaching claim

Keep at least three difficult real examples for the final README.
